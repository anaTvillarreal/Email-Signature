Set Up Your Email Signature

Fill in your details, add your photo, and copy your signature into Gmail. It takes about five minutes.

1

Add Your Details

Type it however you like—we’ll format it as 000.000.0000.

Check your phone number—it looks too short.

Leave it blank to use the Wayfinder demo page.

That doesn’t look like a link. Check that it starts with https://

2

Make Your Circle Photo

Choose a headshot. A photo where you’re centered works best.

Your photo stays on your computer—nothing is uploaded.

Drag the photo to position your face in the circle.

3

Copy Your Signature

Preview

Add your name, title, and phone number to copy your signature.

The preview shows your photo. When you paste, you’ll see a placeholder instead—you’ll swap in your photo in step 4.

4

Add It to Gmail

  1. Open Gmail signature settings
    Scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Name it anything, like “Wayfinder.”
  2. Click inside the signature box and paste (⌘ V on Mac, Ctrl V on Windows).
  3. Click the placeholder photo once, then press Delete. Leave your cursor where the photo was.
  4. Click the Insert image icon in the toolbar, choose Upload, and select the circle photo you downloaded.
  5. Click your photo in the signature box and choose Small.
  6. Under Signature defaults, pick your new signature for new emails and replies.
  7. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save Changes.
Tip: Send yourself a test email to check it. If something looks off, delete the signature in Gmail and start again from step 3.
